Rewards

BTC you earned — THNDR prizes, sats-back, interest. Counted as income at the market price the day it arrived; that value is also your cost basis.

Received

Gifts

BTC someone gave you. Not taxable to receive — its basis is the giver's original cost if you know it, otherwise the market price that day.

Disposals

Sells

What you sold, the proceeds, and what you reported for taxes.

⌕
Given away

Sends

Bitcoin you gave to others — gifts, tips. Moves to another of your own stacks are Transfers, not sends. The coins and their cost basis leave your stack, with no taxable gain. Moves to your own wallets are Transfers (the Transfers pill above); merchant payments are taxable and save as Sells.

Control

Data & settings

Everything is stored locally in this browser. Back it up regularly. Settings here apply to every stack — price, appearance, unit, encryption and backups are set once and shared. The tools further down act on one stack, and say which.

Current BTC price

Used across the dashboard for value & unrealized P/L. Type it in manually — or fetch the live spot price (the only optional network call this app ever makes; it sends none of your data). Shared by every stack: fetch it once and they all use it.

$

Biometric unlock

Unlock with Touch ID (Mac) or Face ID (iPhone/iPad) instead of typing your passphrase. The authenticator never holds your key: it returns a device-bound secret that seals a copy of the vault key on this device only. Your passphrase keeps working everywhere and is still the only key on a new device. If you change your passphrase, re-enable this.

Daily price history

Fetch BTC's daily closing price from your first purchase to today, once, and cache it in your encrypted vault. The value-vs-cost chart, drawdown, house-money and two more Nerd stats then use real daily prices instead of only the days you transacted. Sends only dates; never your data. Never runs on its own — you press the button.

Appearance

Match your mood. Defaults to your system setting. Applies to every stack.

Display unit

Show amounts in BTC or sats (1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats) everywhere. The small sub-line always shows the other unit, and every figure is exact to the sat. You enter amounts in BTC either way.

Health check

Audits every stack against the lot engine — wallet balances vs. holdings, cross-stack moves with both halves intact, lot allocations, orphaned or duplicated records. Runs entirely on this device, reads only, changes nothing. Worth running after any update.

Edit from your iPhone or iPad

Pair a device and you can add and edit entries on it. Those changes travel as an encrypted queue that only this Mac can open — a paired phone can never write your ledger directly, and the server can't read either one. This Mac stays the only writer. Stacks, passphrase, CSV import and bulk edits remain Mac-only.

Stacks

Separate ledgers under one lock — e.g. yours vs. one you share with your wife. Each stack has its own transactions, wallets, lots and taxes, so ownership never blurs. Switch with the picker in the top bar; backups always include every stack.

Cost-basis method

Specific identification, HIFO by default. Sold or gifted coins come from your highest-cost lots first — the allocation that minimizes taxable gains. When recording a sell you can still pick exact lots or switch to FIFO/LIFO; anything without an explicit pick (CSV imports, gifts sent) uses HIFO automatically. Unsold lots keep their original basis, so your remaining stack is always exact.

Encryption

Your node

Verify your ledger against the blockchain using your own node — the Electrum server on your Start9, reached over Tailscale. Your addresses go to your node and nowhere else; a public block explorer would learn your whole wallet, so none is ever used. Then Wallets → Verify on-chain compares every wallet's recorded balance with what the chain actually holds.

Backup — export

Download a full JSON snapshot of everything. Keep it somewhere safe; this is your data.

Import transactions from CSV

Upload an exchange export (Strike, Coinbase, Kraken…) or your own spreadsheet. Purchases, sales and sends are auto-detected and their fees, destinations and descriptions captured; fiat-only deposits/withdrawals are skipped. Map the columns, preview, and import.

Restore — import JSON backup

Load a full JSON backup. This replaces everything currently in the app.

Bootstrap pre-history

Have old buys that predate any records? Give an estimated first-buy date, total invested, and BTC acquired — this creates an evenly-spaced series of estimated buys that sums exactly to your totals, each clearly tagged as backfill.

Mass update existing entries

Find entries already in your ledger by text — e.g. “THNDR Prize” — and update them in bulk: reclassify how they were acquired, flip send ownership, and auto-fill missing cost basis from the historical BTC price on each entry's date.

Load sample data

Kick the tires with a few example transactions so you can see it in action. Won't touch real data if you already have some.

Erase this stack

Permanently deletes all buys, sells, sends, transfers and wallets in the current stack. Other stacks are untouched. Export a backup first.
